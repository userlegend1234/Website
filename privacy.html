Privacy Policy

We take the protection of your personal data seriously. This privacy policy explains which personal data we process when you visit our website or contact us, and for what purpose. It is based on the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Controller

OGURION, [Street and number], [Postcode, City], Switzerland
E-mail: info@ogurion.com

2. Website visits and server log files

When you visit this website, our hosting provider automatically collects technical information such as IP address, date and time of access, the page visited, browser type and operating system. This data is needed to deliver the website securely and is deleted after a short period.

3. Contact form and e-mail

If you contact us via the contact form, your e-mail program opens and your message is sent to us by e-mail. We process the details you provide (name, e-mail address, company, message) exclusively to handle your request. The data is not passed on to third parties without your consent.

4. Fonts

This website uses fonts provided by Google Fonts (Google Ireland Ltd.). When a page is loaded, your browser connects to Google servers and transmits your IP address. Alternatively, the fonts can be hosted locally on our own server.

5. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use tracking cookies or analytics tools.

6. Your rights

You have the right to request information about your personal data stored by us, as well as its correction, deletion or restriction of processing, and to object to processing. Please contact us at the address above.

7. Changes

We may update this privacy policy at any time. The version published on this website applies.
